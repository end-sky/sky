        About Sky

        Sky is a lightweight weather website with a calm, Material-style interface. It shows the current conditions, an hourly outlook, a seven-day forecast, wind, humidity, pressure, UV index, air quality, moon phase and sunrise and sunset for any place on Earth.

        Everything runs in your browser using free, open APIs. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, MET Norway or wttr.in, and you can switch between them. Place search works like a small metasearch engine: it asks Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon together and merges the results.

        No accounts, no cookies and no tracking. Your chosen place, theme and units are stored only in your browser.
